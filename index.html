OUR STORY

Where Tradition Meets the Grill.

Bikes & Barrels — Biryani N Grill is more than a restaurant. It’s a modern Indian dining experience born in Smyrna, GA, plating up the fiery flavors of Hyderabad in a formal, colorful dining room. From our slow-cooked Dum Biryanis to smoky tandoor kebabs, every dish carries the heritage of the subcontinent and the passion of our chefs.

Whether you’re dropping by for a midnight biryani special or booking our private dining room for a celebration — expect generous portions, authentic flavors, and a warm welcome.
